# Finding a Grid

Finding an MGRS coordinate always follows the same process.

## Step 1

Find the correct grid square.

---

## Step 2

Read the Easting.

Move:

**Right**

until you reach the correct value.

---

## Step 3

Read the Northing.

Move:

**Up**

until you reach the correct value.

Remember:

> Right, then Up.

---

## Step 4

The intersection is your location.

That's all there is to it.

Every MGRS coordinate works exactly the same way.
