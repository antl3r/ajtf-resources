# Rapid Mission Planning

Most missions don't need a twenty-minute briefing.

A good squad only needs everyone to understand the same plan.

Keep it simple.

---

## The Planning Cycle

Every mission follows the same three questions.

1. How do we get there?
2. What are we doing?
3. How do we leave?

If everyone can answer those questions, you're ready to move.

---

## Don't Overplan

No plan survives contact with the enemy.

Instead of trying to predict everything, agree on:

- the starting plan
- immediate actions
- who makes decisions

The rest can be adapted during the mission.

---

## One Leader, Shared Understanding

Only one person needs to build the plan.

Everyone else needs to understand it.

A short briefing that everyone remembers is better than a detailed briefing nobody remembers.
