# Contingencies

Every plan should answer one final question.

"What if something goes wrong?"

You don't need ten backup plans.

Usually, answering these is enough.

---

## If We Take Contact Early

Do we:

- continue?
- bypass?
- break contact?

---

## If We Miss the LZ

Where do we regroup?

---

## If Someone Gets Separated

What rally point should they move to?

---

## If Extraction Fails

Where is the alternate pickup point?

---

A contingency doesn't have to solve every problem.

It simply gives everyone the same first reaction.
