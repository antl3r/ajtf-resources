# Common Mistakes

## Explaining Every Detail

<font color="red">Wrong</font>

A ten-minute lecture.

<font color="green">Correct</font>

A two-minute briefing everyone remembers.

---

## No Extraction Plan

<font color="red">Wrong</font>

"We'll figure it out later."

<font color="green">Correct</font>

Primary and alternate extraction locations are known before stepping off.

---

## No Rally Point

<font color="red">Wrong</font>

Separated players don't know where to go.

<font color="green">Correct</font>

Everyone knows where to regroup if contact is lost.

---

## No Questions

<font color="red">Wrong</font>

People stay silent because the briefing ends immediately.

<font color="green">Correct</font>

Take a few seconds to answer any questions before moving.
