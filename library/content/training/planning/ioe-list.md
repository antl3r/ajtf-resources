# The IOE Checklist

Before leaving, every squad should quickly review:

## I — Infiltration

How are we getting there?

Discuss:

- insertion method
- landing zone or start point
- initial movement
- immediate actions after insertion

Everyone should know:

> "Where do we start?"

---

## O — Objective

What are we actually doing?

Examples:

- rescue hostage
- destroy cache
- capture building
- gather intelligence

Also decide:

- who is responsible for what
- when the objective is considered complete

Everyone should know:

> "What does success look like?"

---

## E — Exfiltration

How do we leave?

Discuss:

- extraction point
- extraction method
- rally point if separated

Everyone should know:

> "Where do we go when it's over?"
