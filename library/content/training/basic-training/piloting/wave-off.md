# Wave-Off

A helicopter wave-off is an urgent command or visual signal to stop a landing, increase power, and fly away. It is used when the landing area is unsafe due to obstacles, the craft is taking fire, or the approach was interfered with.

::: tip
Both air crew and ground units may advise a craft to "wave off" for its own safety, and a pilot may announce "waving off" to mean that a craft is breaking contact.
:::
