# Hovering

Hovering refers to the act of remaining *in the air, with neither translational velocity nor altitudinal changes.* In essence, you are not moving.

::: tip
For this, the auto-hover functionality on your craft is usually sufficient to achieve stable hovers.
:::

The purpose of hovering is often observation, providing stability for a door gunner, hovering landings (landings performed without touching the ground), or Holding (to remain within an area while waiting for further instructions), among others.
