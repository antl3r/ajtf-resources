# Radio Quick Reference

## Basic Call

```text
[WHO], THIS IS [YOU]
```

---

## Enemy

```text
Enemy [size] [location]
```

Example:

```text
Enemy fireteam north of church.
```

---

## Casualty

```text
One casualty.

Need medic.
```

---

## Movement

```text
Moving to [location].
```

---

## Completion

```text
Objective secure.

Holding position.
```

---

## Request

```text
Need smoke.

Need ammunition.

Need extraction.

Need support.
```

---

## Before You Transmit

- Who needs this?
- Can I say it shorter?
- Did I include the location?
- Does someone need to act on this?
