# Radio Communication

Good radio communication wins fights.

The goal is **not** to sound military.

The goal is to make sure everyone understands:
- **who** is talking
- **who** they're talking to
- **what** they need
- **where** it is
- **what happens next**

If everyone can consistently do those five things, your radios are good.
