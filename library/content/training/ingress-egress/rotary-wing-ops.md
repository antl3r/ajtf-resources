# Rotary Wing Mounting & Dismounting

Helicopters are at their most vulnerable while on the ground.

Your job is simple:

- Get on quickly.
- Get off quickly.
- Don't create confusion.
- Let the aircraft leave.

---

## The Airhead

An **airhead** is the temporary secure area established around the helicopter after landing.

Its only purpose is to allow troops to safely:

- dismount
- organize
- depart the landing zone

The airhead exists only as long as necessary.

Once everyone is moving toward the objective, the airhead no longer matters.

---

# Before Landing

Everyone should already know:

- which helicopter they're boarding
- where they're sitting
- what happens immediately after landing

Do **not** decide these things after touching down.

---

# Dismounting

## 1. Wait for the Aircraft

Unless directed otherwise:

Remain seated until the helicopter has landed.

Don't jump early.

---

## 2. Exit Immediately

As soon as the aircraft has landed:

- leave the aircraft
- move several meters away
- make room for everyone behind you

Don't stop next to the helicopter.

---

## 3. Clear the Landing Zone

The helicopter may need to:

- take off immediately
- reposition
- land another aircraft

Move away from the aircraft before stopping.

---

## 4. Establish the Airhead

Once clear of the helicopter:

- orient yourself
- identify nearby teammates
- cover likely enemy approaches

You are providing security for everyone still unloading.

---

## 5. Move to the Objective

Once everyone has dismounted:

Leave the airhead.

Don't remain near the landing zone unless specifically ordered.
