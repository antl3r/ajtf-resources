# Rotary Wing Quick Reference

## Insertion

1. Stay seated.
2. Land.
3. Exit immediately.
4. Clear the helicopter.
5. Establish the airhead.
6. Cover the landing zone.
7. Move to the objective.

---

## Extraction

1. Secure the landing zone.
2. Wait for the helicopter.
3. Board quickly.
4. Fill seats without blocking doors.
5. Stay seated.
6. Depart.

---

## Remember

The helicopter is never the objective.

Don't stop beside it.

Don't block it.

Don't delay it.
