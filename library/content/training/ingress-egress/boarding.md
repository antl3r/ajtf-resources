# Boarding Procedures

A helicopter should spend as little time on the ground as possible.

The fastest boarding is an organized boarding.

---

# Before Boarding

Be ready.

- weapon lowered
- correct helicopter identified
- teammates nearby
- no unnecessary movement

If you aren't boarding yet, stay clear of the aircraft.

---

# Board Quickly

Move directly to the aircraft.

Avoid:

- stopping
- turning around
- checking equipment
- chatting

Once inside:

Take your seat.

---

# Don't Block the Door

If you're inside:

Move away from the entrance.

Allow the next player to board immediately.

---

# Stay Seated

Remain seated until:

- instructed otherwise
- the helicopter lands

Moving around inside the aircraft creates confusion.
