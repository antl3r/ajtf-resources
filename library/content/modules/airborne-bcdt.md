# Airborne Basic Combat & Deployment Training

## Equipment
- Flash grenades
- White smokes
- Green smokes
- Red smokes

## Weaponry
- Primary: Assault, Carbine, Battle rifle
- Secondary: Of operators choice.
- Melee: Of operators choice (halligan bar must be brought by designated breacher)

#  [CQB PROTOCOL]

## Basic Comms
"Stack/Double stack on me"
"'Banshee' Hold rear"
"Ready Flash"
"Flash ready"
"Flash out/flash hot"
"MOVE MOVE MOVE"

# Breaching Procedure
- When designated point man (assigned by squad lead) orders "stack on me" you all stack on one side off his position
- When designated point man (assigned by squad lead) orders "double stack on me" you evenly stack on opposite sides of the door
- If designated point man yells your callsign and orders you to hold rear you keep a constant sight on the rear at the end of the stack
- When designated point man yells flash ready, operator behind him readies flash to be thrown
- When signaled by designated point man you then throw flash and call "flash out/flash hot"
- Once flash goes off, designated point man will yell MOVE MOVE MOVE, all units must enter quickly following the designated route for CQB procedures (will be explained in more advanced trainings)

(IF YOU ARE NOT DESIGNATED POINT MAN CLEAR COMMS UNLESS REPORTING IMPORTANT INFORMATION SO ALL UNITS UNDERSTAND AND CAN CLEARLY HEAR INSTRUCTIONS)
### Keep practicing until Training Officer is happy with all units CQB skills ###
