# Uniforms

Refer to uniforms in the individual tabs

**Disciplinary actions will occur if correct uniform is not worn during events, or training (unless officer teaching clears with Air Marshal, or Vice Air Marshal otherwise)**

During any other time, whether its public, or playing with others in the server you may use whatever you'd like
## Disciplinary actions may include:
<br/>First offense:  
Verbal warning  
<br/>Second offense:  
Written warning  
<br/>3rd offense:  
Demotion (Ranks can differ so long as its reasonable)  
<br/>4th offense:  
Council meeting, and potential kick from the faction
::: tip
If you do not have the money to buy the uniform, this has to be expressed before the event, as we understand some users have limitations.
:::
